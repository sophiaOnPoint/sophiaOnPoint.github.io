I will be a postdoc working on computational imaging and AI for science, hosted by Prof. Polina Golland at MIT CSAIL and Dr. Ellen Grant at Boston Children's Hospital.


I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from MIT, advised by Prof. Dirk Englund at MIT EECS, RLE, and MTL. My dissertation focused on atomically-precise characterization of quantum devices through physics-informed modeling. I obtained my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University, where I worked closely with Prof. Peide Ye and Prof. Supriyo Datta on semiconductor devices. I have also interned at NTT Research Lab (2024).


Research Interests:  I am broadly interested in applying physical laws — such as ray tracing in optics and acoustics — into computational frameworks to better understand complex physical systems. My work sits at the intersection of computational methods, medical imaging, and quantum optics.
