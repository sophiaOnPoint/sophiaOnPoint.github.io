Vertically Loaded Diamond Microdisk Resonator (VLDMoRt)


VLDMoRt is a vertically loaded diamond microdisk resonator designed to enable scalable spin–photon interfaces for free-space quantum network architectures. Although optimized cavity designs can enhance light–matter interaction, their performance is highly sensitive to nanometer-scale deviations between the intended geometry and the fabricated device. To address this design–fabrication gap, we developed a physics-informed neural-network framework that reconstructs device geometry from SEM and AFM measurements, builds simulation models of the realized cavity, and predicts spin–photon interface performance. By closing the loop between fabrication metrology, electromagnetic simulation, and design optimization, this approach provides a robust pathway toward scalable, fabrication-tolerant diamond cavities.
