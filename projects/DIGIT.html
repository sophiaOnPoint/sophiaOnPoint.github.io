Discrete-grid Imaging Technique (DIGIT)


Super-resolution microscopy is an essential tool for studying fundamental physical and biological mechanisms, but its localization performance remains limited by both photon statistics and model fidelity. To overcome these limits, we developed a physics-informed computational microscopy framework that combines a multiphysics digital twin of super-resolution imaging with a Bayesian inference framework that incorporates material constraints such as underlying lattice structure, and defect priors. By converting physical knowledge of both the microscope and the material into quantitative inference constraints, this approach achieves record-setting atomic localization precision and Ångström-level localization accuracy.
